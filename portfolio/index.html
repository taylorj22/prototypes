How I Work
Design QA
Reduced Design QA load to under 10%

Design QA was consuming a significant chunk of my time. I introduced twice-weekly office hours where engineers can share where they're at and get feedback in real time, replacing reactive back-and-forth with a predictable, lightweight touchpoint. It cut the load without cutting the quality.

Process
Mini design jams with engineers

I introduced a new working format: small sessions with Proposals engineers early in the design process, before specs were finalized. The goal was to surface the "why" earlier and catch edge cases before handoff. Questions that used to surface in QA started getting resolved in the jam instead.

Tooling
Daily customer feedback digest

I built and run a bot that surfaces keyword-matched feedback from #suggestions and #customer_feedback on a daily cadence. Self-initiated tooling to stay closer to customer signal than the role formally requires.

AI Practice
Claude skills for the design team

I built and released two Claude skills for the team: the States Audit Skill and the Roofr Formatting Skill. They automate the repetitive, error-prone work that generated the most back-and-forth with engineers before, during, and after handoff. I also ran an AI Skill Share workshop with the proposals team to improve team-wide workflows.

Engineering Process
Shaping Spec Driven Development

I've been working with Robert on the Architecture team to understand what SDD requires from design, flagging it as a systemic issue in upcoming tickets and actively shaping what high-quality PRDs and prototypes need to look like to support it. Work that lives at the boundary of design, engineering, and process.

Handoff
Code prototypes as the spec

For smaller, well-scoped work I skip Figma entirely and hand off working code prototypes directly to engineering, cutting the design-to-dev cycle significantly. Adding a line item to a specific section, renaming estimate options, and saving proposals as a template all shipped with zero Figma screens. The prototype is the spec.
