About Elite Team Roofers

Elite Team Roofers has been serving the Lower Mainland since 2004. We're a family-owned operation built on the belief that every homeowner deserves a roof they can count on — installed right, the first time.

We hold GAF Master Elite certification (top 2% of roofers in Canada) and carry full WCB coverage and $5M liability insurance. Our crews are in-house, not subcontracted, so quality is consistent on every job we take.

We've completed over 3,400 residential and commercial roofing projects across Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley, and the Tri-Cities. Our average Google rating is 4.9/5 across 800+ reviews.

Terms & Conditions

Payment: 30% deposit due upon signing. Remaining balance due upon project completion. We accept e-transfer, cheque, Visa, and Mastercard. A 2.8% processing fee applies to credit card payments.

Scheduling: Work will be scheduled within 10–14 business days of deposit receipt. Weather delays may affect the start date. We will notify you of any changes no later than 24 hours in advance.

Scope of work: This proposal covers the scope outlined above. Any additional work discovered during the project (e.g. rotted decking, damaged fascia) will be documented, quoted, and approved before proceeding.

Clean-up: All debris will be removed from the property daily. A magnetic sweep will be performed after the job is complete. We carry waste manifest documentation for all material disposal.

Cancellation: If the project is cancelled after deposit, a 15% administration fee will be deducted from any refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of the scheduled start date forfeit the full deposit.
